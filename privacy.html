Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Nudge is a follow-up and deal-tracking app for real estate agents. This policy explains what information Nudge handles and how. The short version: your clients' information stays on your device. We only store the basic account details needed to sign you in.

Information stored in your account

When you sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we use Google Firebase to create and manage your account. We store:

  • Your email address and name (if provided by you or your sign-in provider)
  • Which sign-in method you used
  • When your account was created and when you last signed in

If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive only the private relay address Apple provides.

Information that stays on your device

The clients, contacts, key dates (such as birthdays and home anniversaries), deals, tasks, notes, and message templates you add to Nudge are stored locally on your device. We do not upload them to our servers. They may be included in your device's own backups (for example, iCloud Backup), which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Contacts

If you choose to import from iPhone Contacts, Nudge asks for permission first and reads only the contacts you make available. Imported details are saved on your device only. You can turn off access at any time in Settings.

Files and Google Sheets

If you import a CSV file or a Google Sheets link, Nudge reads that file or sheet on your device to create contacts. Sheets are downloaded directly from Google using the link you provide; we do not receive or store the file.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Their content is created on your device and is not sent to us.

Messages, calls, and email

When you text, call, or email a client from Nudge, the app opens your device's Messages, Phone, or Mail app. We do not see or store those communications.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your information or your clients' information.
  • We do not show ads or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to provide sign-in and store your account details. Google processes this information on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy. Signing in with Apple or Google is also subject to those companies' privacy policies.

Keeping your information

We keep your account details while your account exists. Information stored on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Settings and tap Delete account at the bottom. This permanently deletes your account and the account details we store, and erases your Nudge data from that device.

If you can't access the app, email us at barenchi.adam@gmail.com from the address on your account, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Nudge is intended for professionals and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

We rely on the security protections of Apple's platform and Google Firebase. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect the information we hold.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email barenchi.adam@gmail.com.
